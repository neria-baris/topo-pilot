# E2: exploratory notes (NOT pre-registered)

Written by the conductor on 2026-10-13, after `summary.md` was generated and read for the first time. Nothing here changes a verdict, threshold, or rule. Each item is a reading of numbers already in `summary.md`, or a follow-up idea. A follow-up needs its own pre-registration before it counts as evidence.

1. **R1 (embedding of the last symbol) carries a large part of the F1 contrast under O.** R1's median D per replicate is 0.140 / 0.083 / 0.094, which is 0.89 / 0.47 / 0.54 of the model's median D, with D > 0 in 8/8 panels in every replicate. The pre-registered mixing rule (RESEARCH_SUMMARY §7.6) covers only R2 and R3, which stay at 0.17–0.20 and 0.02–0.10. R1 falls outside that rule, but it suggests the learned symbol embeddings already hold much of the emission geometry: the 36 symbol centres are periodic in x on the cylinder. This is what condition P (S5, H3/H3′) is designed to separate out. Under O, F1 alone does not show that the structure comes from history integration and not from emission geometry.
2. **History integration is clear despite (1).** Within pairs that share the last symbol, ρ_s(d_A, d_B32) has a per-run median of 0.935–0.979. It is positive in every panel, and it beats R2 and R3 in 48/48 run-panels for both V0 and V1 (X4-d). R1 is constant on those pairs, so this part cannot come from the last-symbol embedding.
3. **Normalisation raises the contrast.** The median D under V1 (0.28–0.30) is 1.6–1.8× the median D under V0 (0.16–0.18). The verdicts agree (X4-a), but the raw metric understates the signal. Following caveat 4, this shows sensitivity to normalisation and says nothing about where the signal lies.
4. **The readout-null part V4 also carries the contrast.** The F1 rule holds under V4 (SUPPORTED), with median D at 0.39–0.56 of V3's. Category "both"; the interpretation follows caveat 2.
5. **Reporting artefact.** The prediction table shows the N run's learning gate as "fail" because G_C = 0 makes "captured" undefined. The N model is judged by H0, which passes; the gate column should read "n/a" for N. Fixed before README E2: the N row now shows "n/a" for captured and for the learning gate.
