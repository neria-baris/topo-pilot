## Common harness: modular dependencies and stages

The [eight-layer design](RESEARCH_HARNESS_ARCHITECTURE.md) separates world, process, task/oracle, learner, property, estimator, intervention and report. Its six scientific gates preserve upstream limitations. It uses existing contracts/modules/access policy rather than adding general superclasses.

Architectural Stage1 implements B only first; C/D adapters are conditional. Stage2 chooses X5 G1 as the proposed first stress test, reusing HMM/reference/learning evaluation while adapting metric/T. Stage3 defers transport/base-fiber and cover/stalk/restriction/cochain machinery until a selected observable task warrants it. If delaying an abstraction simplifies X4 without materially increasing later cost, delay it.

M1–M3/M7 are enough for B. M4–M6 support C; M8 is D only. Future task-specific oracle adapters can reuse reporting without promising a universal oracle. World invariants, PH statistics, curvature and section obstructions are never interchangeable scalar scores. Architectural reuse does not make different tasks' raw losses scientifically comparable.
