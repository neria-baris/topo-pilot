# Topo Pilot: research landscape

Planning snapshot: 13 October 2026, Asia/Jerusalem. Repository commit `f8afc2a5e43309aa4bf2d402f2be4e38470df180`. **Proposal only; no experiments, training, or implementation performed.** Literature cutoff is 13 October, not the remainder of October 2026.

## Executive assessment

The useful next question is whether a learned representation preserves *future-relevant distinctions that its immediate output discards*, and whether a specified downstream computation uses those distinctions. A detected loop is not itself an explanation of prediction. The immediate programme should audit predictive equivalence and the effective readout null space, then test horizon-specific interventions on frozen models. Geometry and boundary experiments are complementary, with explicit identifiability gates.

Generic belief decoding, circular representations, and manifold steering already have close precedents. The plausible contribution is a controlled separation of predictive information, finite-scale topology, and causal computation, with an exact stochastic oracle and independent evaluation histories. No priority or publication claim is justified yet.

Throughout this package: **[R]** verified repository finding; **[M]** established mathematics or an argument given here; **[L]** literature result in its stated setting; **[H]** proposed falsifiable hypothesis; **[S]** speculation. These hypotheses are post hoc relative to completed E3/E4 and become prospective only under a frozen new protocol and independent evaluation. Literature findings, established theorems, pilot results, hypotheses and speculation remain separate.

## Five separate scientific claims and an independent generalization axis

| Question | Object and test | What does not answer it |
|---|---|---|
| Predictive information | Equivalence of joint future laws on reachable beliefs | Known world Betti numbers or failure of one decoder |
| Representation | Held-out recovery under declared affine/nonlinear families | Accurate output alone or causal use |
| Geometry/topology | Calibrated metric organization, PH or a typed invariant | Probe R² or raw finite-cloud clusters |
| Computation | Frozen suffix intervention with damage/support controls | PH/circular correspondence or auxiliary Q2 decoding |
| Optimization | Paired learning curves relative to each process's oracle | Raw CE or an unexplained convergence failure |

Generalization is an additional evaluation axis requiring frozen fresh/rare/shifted histories and an appropriate oracle. It cannot be inferred from a representation or optimization result alone.

The central open question is **when representations retain globally structured information beyond immediate prediction, and when a specified computation uses it to improve prediction or generalization**. E4 makes generic belief decoding or a ring plot less compelling as a contribution and makes exact-F1, R1 and capacity controls more important.

## Mathematical points already settled

**[M] Predictive equivalence.** Let `D_w=diag(E[:,w])`, with emit-then-transition alignment and posterior row vector b after the current emission. For a future word s=(s1,...,sk),

\[
Q_k(b)[s]=bT D_{s_1}T D_{s_2}\cdots T D_{s_{k-1}}T E_{:,s_k}.
\]

Write this linear map as bGk; G1=TE. Then `b ~k b'` iff `(b-b')Gk=0`. Equality of length-k joint laws implies equality at every shorter horizon by marginalization. Equality of all marginal token laws `b T^j E` does **not** imply equality of joint futures. The predictive state is an equivalence class of histories; hidden-state labels are not uniquely identified by it. This is the connection to [Singh, James and Rudary's PSRs](https://web.eecs.umich.edu/~baveja/Papers/uai2004psr.pdf).

**[M] Longer horizons can distinguish one-step-equivalent beliefs.** Such a pair exists when a sum-zero direction v satisfies `vG1=0` but `vG2!=0` and feasible beliefs b±εv lie in the simplex. An interior b ensures feasibility for small ε. It does not ensure these beliefs are reachable at context 32. Rank on the full simplex and discrimination on actually reachable beliefs must both be checked. Numerical small singular values are not proof of exact equivalence.

An explicit algebraic example uses a four-state path, symmetric max-degree lazy walk with neighbor probability0.4, and deterministic emissions A at states0/1/2 and B at state3. Beliefs concentrated at state0 or state1 both predict A with probability1 at the next step. At two steps, the state0 belief predicts AA with probability1, whereas the state1 belief predicts AB with probability0.16. This proves possibility within a symmetric stochastic kernel, not reachability or a result for the pilot's O/P process. Separately, joint laws assigning half mass to AA/BB versus half to AB/BA have equal first/second marginals and different correlations; marginal comparisons alone cannot establish joint predictive equivalence.

**[M] Sufficiency does not imply world-topology preservation.** If E has identical rows, all histories have the same future law regardless of world topology. More generally, T and E can depend only on the 1-skeleton, leaving changes to filled higher cells observationally invisible. Recovery of those cells requires a restricted world family or additional assumptions. Recovering a minimal predictive process does not uniquely recover an arbitrary latent complex.

**[M] Conditional topology statement.** On a compact reachable belief set K, a continuous Qk that is injective is a homeomorphism onto Qk(K), because its target is Hausdorff. This statement concerns K, not the original world. The full belief simplex is contractible, and a finite sampled cloud is not literally a manifold. Finite-scale VR features can change under distortion even when an underlying embedding remains homeomorphic.

**[M] Readout and gauge.** For final residual z,

\[
u_\epsilon(z)=\frac{z-\bar z\mathbf1}{\sqrt{\operatorname{var}(z)+\epsilon}},\quad
\ell=uA+c,\quad A=\operatorname{diag}(\gamma)W_U^\top.
\]

With C=I−11ᵀ/V, softmax depends on `uAC`. Thus the logit-visible projector uses range(A), while the effective projector uses range(AC). A perturbation δu with δuAC=0 preserves probabilities exactly, at the **post-normalization affine readout**, including constant-logit shifts. Re-normalizing a perturbed raw z invalidates that conclusion. Mean shifts are exact LN gauges; positive scaling is exact only for ε=0. For ε>0, `uε(αz)=uε/α²(z)` for α>0. These are algebraic controls, not discoveries.

**[M] Same output means same one-step risk.** If an intervention preserves p(w|h) pointwise, oracle-relative one-step KL cannot change. Final-readout-null ablations test redundancy of stored representations, not the necessity of topology for the unchanged output. A functional test needs another computation, an earlier layer, or a different horizon. In this Transformer, the post-final-LN vector is not recurrent state or a KV cache.

**[L/M] Stability is conditional.** Persistence stability controls diagrams under uniform perturbations of appropriate filtrations. Average KL alone gives no uniform pointwise cloud-distance bound. [Cohen-Steiner, Edelsbrunner and Harer](https://scholars.duke.edu/publication/696545) therefore do not imply that a learning gate guarantees the model's H1. Sampling/reach assumptions in [Niyogi, Smale and Weinberger](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00454-008-9053-2) must not be transplanted to history clouds without verification.

### Where the shared harness sits relative to prior work

Predictive-state/HMM belief work supplies the notion of future-equivalent information, not proof that the pilot learned its latent complex. World-model coherence and recent manifold interventions are close empirical precedents; novelty must lie in a precise controlled horizon/function test, not “a Transformer has geometry.” Geometric deep learning organizes architectural symmetries/inductive biases; this programme first examines representations of an unchanged architecture. Topological deep learning and sheaf neural networks explicitly use cells/restrictions in models; using such mathematics in a generator/evaluator does not make our Transformer a sheaf network. [Hansen–Ghrist](https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.01513) and [Neural Sheaf Diffusion](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2022/hash/75c45fca2aa416ada062b26cc4fb7641-Abstract-Conference.html) provide precise machinery and prior art, not evidence of learned gluing obstruction.

Mechanistic interpretability contributes interventions and their failure modes; controls must distinguish generic information damage from topology changes. Sampling/reach/persistence theorems require their assumptions; they do not certify correlated finite activation clouds. The [harness](RESEARCH_HARNESS_ARCHITECTURE.md) standardizes scientific gates/provenance while retaining these property-specific assumptions. It is an architectural proposal, not an independent contribution claim.

## Genuine gaps and ideas to stop pursuing

**[H] Testable gap:** Do final effective-null representations retain linearly accessible joint-future information beyond oracle F1, and can an earlier-layer intervention selectively impair its use? Prior future-information and intervention papers substantially overlap; the specific controlled comparison is the contribution candidate, not an established novelty claim.

**[H] Testable gap:** At fixed topology and occupancy, does a defined metric-dependent process alter oracle-relative learning time? Curvature can motivate the process; a two-condition result estimates the effect of that process, not curvature in isolation.

**[H] Testable gap:** Does calibrated local boundary organization survive the map from oracle beliefs to activations after controlling for history sampling and symbol statistics? This extends the pilot's global H1 focus and might fail at the oracle stage.

**[S] Longer-term gap:** Can distinguishable transported predictive information expose orientation or cycle consistency, rather than just geometry of observed points? A meaningful operational model and observability proof are dependencies.

Abandon as discovery targets: G0 loss differences under exactly the same observation law; exact readout-null invariance; cylinder/Möbius discrimination by ordinary Betti numbers; equating absent H1 with contractibility; inferring local minima from failed convergence; claiming pure topology causation from a single eraser; claiming a gluing obstruction for restrictions of one global affine readout. These are invalid or mathematically predetermined questions.

This is a focused search, not an exhaustive review or replication audit. Search families covered HMM beliefs/PSRs, world coherence, causal erasure/patching/manifold steering, learnability/curvature, local homology, orientation/characteristic classes, persistent homotopy, and sheaf diffusion, including 2025–2026 work. ## Updated novelty and conclusion boundary

H4C-I remains conditional and tests linear accessibility; the mandatory nonlinear-Q1 comparator addresses an important alternative without changing its primary≥10% rule. Strict additional information beyond Q1 needs adequate reachable equivalence evidence, not an unsuccessful MLP. H4C-F can test coordinate-associated use; no pure topology-specific intervention has been demonstrated. X5 can compare specified process learning difficulty; isolated curvature causation remains unestablished. X6 boundary recovery is primarily feasibility unless it improves beyond R1; concrete transport now has an analytic observable construction, but no new model result.

The most substantive candidate contribution is a reproducible, horizon-specific computational effect with exact targets, capacity/symbol/damage/support controls and independent replication, or a well-calibrated negative result showing the hypothesized component dispensable under that protocol. Neither a general-purpose harness nor another belief probe alone establishes novelty. Existing theorems and exact invariances should be used as controls, not advertised as discoveries.
