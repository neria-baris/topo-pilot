## Questions raised (SPEC-QUESTION / API-AMBIGUITY)
1. **SPEC-QUESTION (reservation END location).** API.md `reservation_ended` (b) looks for `<dir of res_path>/<id>.END.json`; CONTRACT `window.ended_rule` (precedence over API) and J8 item 2 place it in `<store>/reservations/` also for a top-level reservation (`hwsu-store/RESERVATION*.json`). Tested per CONTRACT/J8 (`test_track_u_top_level_reservation`; fails on the current code).
2. **SPEC-QUESTION (obligation `store` format).** API.md `write_obligation` writes `str(store)` (backslashes on Windows); CONTRACT `obligation.types` and J11 say forward slashes. Tested per J11 (passes on the current code; API text to align).
3. **API-AMBIGUITY (risk boundary).** `registry_risk_d` "equality passes" cannot be reached with a float `p` (1/1,331,200 is not dyadic); tested just above and below.
4. **Defect, not ambiguity.** `read_k4b_table` raises `KeyError` on an npz without `seq_ids`; API.md says any defect → `ValueError` (`test_read_k4b_table` fails on the current code).
5. **Question for the coordinator (not tested).** A preflight refusal records gates into `GATES.jsonl` of the store. J10 item 2 ("every job that writes into a track's store ends by writing an obligation") does not say whether a refused job counts.
6. **Note (DRD13).** Every path under a pytest basetemp contains the J9 fragment `/topo-scratch/test_hwsd-`, so a seed search on a synthetic root inside the basetemp classifies every hit as a declaration. The J9 classification tests therefore use synthetic hit paths, not files.
