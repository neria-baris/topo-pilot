# Research summary: binding decisions

This file is the **binding** research specification. The full rationale lives in `RESEARCH_PLAN_HE.md`, cited below as `[HE §n]`. If the two disagree, this file wins, and the disagreement is logged in `state/DECISIONS.md`.

## 1. Question

Next-token training on sequences generated by a latent world of known topology raises three questions:

1. Does the residual stream acquire structure that reflects that topology?
2. If it does, does that structure come from the geometry of the observations, from beliefs accumulated over the history, or only from what is needed to predict the next token?

We **detect** structure and correlate it with prediction quality. We do **not** claim that the model uses the structure or needs it; that would require interventions, which are an extension.

## 2. Hypotheses and the single finding

| ID | Statement | Measure | Falsified or limited when |
|---|---|---|---|
| **F1 / H1 (primary)** | Training increases a persistent H₁ signal that distinguishes the cylinder from the square | $D_{r,i}$ (§7.4) | Any of the decision-rule conditions in §7.4 fails while all gates passed |
| H2 | Activation geometry follows the 32-symbol belief more than the last-symbol posterior | $\Delta_\text{geom}$, overall and within pairs with the same last symbol (§7.5) | $\Delta_\text{geom}\le 0$, or the effect vanishes within same-last-symbol pairs |
| H3 | Under the axis-permuted emission P, $D_r>0$ still holds, so the structure is learned from dynamics and not only from emission geometry | F1 rule applied to P | The rule fails under P although the learning gate passed |
| H4 | $S$ rises only after the model starts using history (regret drops below $G_C$) | curves over 12 checkpoints; checkpoint-order permutation null | $S$ rises before history use |
| H0 | Under identical emissions nothing about the latent state is learnable | NLL ≥ $H(W)$ within CI; $b\equiv\pi$ | NLL below $H(W)$ indicates leakage or a bug |

## 3. Worlds and dynamics [HE §3]

- **Lattice:** $12\times12$ with unit spacing and 4-neighbourhood, so $N=144$ states with dense ids `0..143`.
- **Square:** no identifications. $\beta=(1,0,0)$, $\chi=1$.
- **Cylinder:** periodic in x. $\beta=(1,1,0)$, $\chi=0$.
- **Ground truth:** computed from the explicit cubical complex (vertices, edges, filled unit squares) using ranks of boundary matrices over $\mathbb{F}_p$. The transition graph's cycle rank is *not* the topology of the surface.
- **Metric:** primary $d_M$ is the intrinsic flat metric, with the periodic minimum in x for the cylinder. The sensitivity metric is graph geodesic distance.
- **Dynamics:** a lazy walk normalised by maximum degree:
  $$T_{ij}=\frac{1-p_\text{stay}}{D_\text{max}}\ \text{for neighbours},\qquad T_{ii}=1-\deg(i)\,\frac{1-p_\text{stay}}{D_\text{max}},$$
  with $p_\text{stay}=0.2$ and $D_\text{max}=4$. $T$ is symmetric and doubly stochastic, so the stationary distribution $\pi$ is uniform. On this lattice the rule is identical to "a blocked move becomes a stay". It also generalises to the extension worlds.

## 4. Emissions [HE §3.3–3.4]

| Code | Definition | Role | Runs |
|---|---|---|---|
| **O** | $V=36$ centres on a fixed $6\times6$ grid, $c_{ab}=(0.5+2a,0.5+2b)$; $E_{zj}\propto\exp(-d_M(z,c_j)^2/2\sigma^2)$; symbol names randomly permuted (seeded) | main | S4 |
| **P** | Axis-wise state permutation of O: $E_P[(x,y)]=E_O[(\sigma_x(x),\sigma_y(y))]$, with $\sigma_x,\sigma_y$ fixed per world (seeded) | matched control: same rows, same $H(W\mid Z)$, same $p(w)$, same product structure; spatial smoothness destroyed | S5 |
| **N** | $E_{zw}=1/36$ | negative control (world-independent, so one model is enough) | S4, S7 |
| **U** | $E=I$ up to a permutation, $V=144$ | sanity control for dynamics learning; NLL not comparable across conditions | S7 |

- **Product structure.** O factorises exactly as $E^x[x,a]\,E^y[y,b]$, which is why P is axis-wise: it breaks smoothness only. A full permutation of all states is an extension.
- **σ calibration (S2, generator only).** Try $\sigma\in(1.25, 0.75, 2.0)$ in that order. Pick the first value that meets all of the following **in both worlds**:
  - mean entropy of $b^{(1)}$ ≥ $\log 2$;
  - $G_C\ge 0.10$ nats;
  - every $p(w)\ge 0.5/V$.

  **Fallback:** take the candidate with the highest $\min_\text{world}G_C$, provided it is ≥ 0.03, and flag the run as "weak context". If no candidate reaches 0.03, stop and go to checkpoint H3.

## 5. Sequences and the exact predictor [HE §4]

- **Generation:** $z_1\sim\pi$; for $t=1..33$, emit $w_t\sim E[z_t]$, then (if $t<33$) transition $z_{t+1}\sim T[z_t]$. Every trajectory is independent and starts from $\pi$.
- **Model input:** `[BOS, w_1..w_32]` (33 positions, p = 0..32). Targets are `[w_1..w_33]`. Position p sees $w_{1:p}$ and predicts $w_{p+1}$.
- **Masking and packing:** a causal mask; no packing, no padding, no cross-sequence attention.
- **Primary analysis position:** $p=32$, one history per trajectory.
- **Filter** (row vectors):
  $$a_1=\pi,\quad b_t=\frac{a_t\odot E_{:,w_t}}{a_tE_{:,w_t}},\quad a_{t+1}=b_tT,\quad q_t=b_tTE,\quad q_0=\pi E.$$
  Use float64. Zero evidence raises `IMPOSSIBLE_HISTORY`.
- **Literature mapping:** Shai et al. use edge emission with $T^{(x)}=\mathrm{diag}(E_{:,x})T$, so their $\eta$ equals our $a_{p+1}$.
- **Last-symbol baseline:** $r_w=\pi\odot E_{:,w}/(\pi E_{:,w})$ and $q_\text{last}=r_{w_p}TE$. We write $b^{(1)}=r_{w_{32}}$ and $b^{(32)}=b_{32}$.
- **Futures:** $F_1=bTE$ and $F_2[i,j]=b\,T\,\mathrm{diag}(E_{:,i})\,T\,E_{:,j}$ (the joint distribution of the next two symbols).
- **Observability:** report the singular values of $O_k$ for $k\le3$.
- **Context gain:** $G_C=\overline{\mathrm{KL}}(q_C\,\|\,q_\text{last})$ at $p=32$.

## 6. Model and training [HE §6]

- **Architecture:** decoder-only, pre-LN; 4 blocks; $d=128$; 4 heads; $d_\text{mlp}=512$; GELU; learned positional embeddings; final LayerNorm; untied unembedding; dropout 0. Input vocabulary $V+1$, output vocabulary $V$. About 0.8M parameters.
- **Hooks:** `embed`, `resid_post_0..3`, `ln_final_out`. The primary hook is `resid_post_3`, before the final LN.
- **Optimiser:** AdamW, lr 1e-3, 200 warmup steps, cosine decay to step 5000, weight decay 0.1, gradient clipping 1.0, batch 128. About 21M target tokens per run.
- **Data per replicate:** train 131,072, dev 4,096, test 4,096 trajectories. Master seeds are 1729, 2718, 3141; the replicate seed changes the data, init, symbol names, and batch order.
- **Checkpoints, always saved:** 0, 50, 100, 200, 400, 700, 1000, 1500, 2000, 3000, 4000, 5000. Primary analysis at 5000.
- **Learning gate:** $\text{captured}=1-\text{regret}/G_C\ge0.8$, where $\text{regret}=\overline{\mathrm{KL}}(q_C\,\|\,p_\text{model})$ at $p=32$.

## 7. Analysis [HE §5, §7]

### 7.1 Panels
Eight disjoint panels of 512 test trajectories each, one history per trajectory at $p=32$. Panels are fixed by a seed and shared across hooks, checkpoints, and spaces. Panel 1 is used for training curves; all 8 are used at checkpoints 0 and 5000.

### 7.2 Spaces and metrics (the same histories in every space)
| Space | Points | Primary metric | Sensitivity |
|---|---|---|---|
| World | 144 states | $d_M$ | graph geodesic |
| Emission field | 144 rows of $E$ | Hellinger | $\sqrt{\mathrm{JSD}}$ |
| Beliefs | $b^{(32)}$, $b^{(1)}$ | Hellinger | $\sqrt{\mathrm{JSD}}$ |
| Futures | $F_1$, $F_2$ | Hellinger | $\sqrt{\mathrm{JSD}}$ |
| Activations | primary hook, $\mathbb{R}^{128}$ | raw Euclidean | chord; PCA-32 fitted on dev; $M=256$ |

- Not allowed: 1−cos, k-NN geodesics, UMAP or t-SNE as evidence, and per-state means in the primary analysis.
- $W_1$ with $d_M$ is allowed only as a labelled sensitivity check.

### 7.3 Persistent homology
- **Filtration:** Vietoris–Rips over $\mathbb{F}_2$ (the coefficient field is a parameter), dimensions 0–1, with a threshold that closes every H₁ bar. Censored bars are flagged.
- **Scale:** $s$ is the 95th percentile of positive pairwise distances.
- **Signal:**
  $$S=\max_{\text{finite H}_1\text{ bars}}(\text{death}-\text{birth})/s,$$
  with $S=0$ when there is no H₁ bar.
- **Also stored:** $\pi_\text{max}=$ death/birth, the top-3 persistences, Betti curves, and full diagrams.
- **Noise floor:** $S$ of isotropic and of covariance-matched Gaussian clouds (same $M$ and dimension, 20 draws each). The floor is reported as context, never used as a threshold.
- **Collapsed cloud:** status `collapsed` with $S=0$.

### 7.4 F1 decision rule
For replicate $r$ and panel $i$ (the same panel index in both worlds):
$$D_{r,i}=(S^{cyl}_{final,i}-S^{cyl}_{init,i})-(S^{sq}_{final,i}-S^{sq}_{init,i}).$$
Let $u_r=\max_{c}\ q_{95}\{|S_{c,i}-S_{c,j}|:i<j\}$ over the four cells $c\in\{$cyl-final, cyl-init, sq-final, sq-init$\}$.

**F1 is supported** iff all of the following hold:
1. $D_{r,i}>0$ for at least 7 of the 8 panels in **each** of the 3 replicates;
2. $\mathrm{median}_i D_{r,i}>u_r$ for each replicate;
3. every model passed the learning gate.

**F1 is rejected** iff all gates passed and condition 1 or 2 fails. **Otherwise the result is inconclusive**, and the report names the failed gate.

The four $S$ values are always reported next to $D$.

### 7.5 H2 measure
On all pairs within a panel:
$$\Delta_\text{geom}=\rho_s(d_A,d_{B32})-\rho_s(d_A,d_{B1}),$$
computed again restricted to pairs with the same $w_{32}$. The decision is the sign consistency across 3 replicates and 8 panels. Pair correlations are never significance tests.

### 7.6 Representation baselines (same pipeline)
- **R0:** the model at initialisation.
- **R1:** the trained model's embedding of the last symbol.
- **R2 (previous-token mixing):** $\tfrac12[\phi(w_{32})+\phi(w_{31})]$, where $\phi$ is the spectral embedding (8–16 components) of the symbol transition matrix $E^\top\mathrm{diag}(\pi)TE$.
- **R3 (context average):** $\tfrac1{32}\sum_t\phi(w_t)$.

If R2 or R3 show $D>0$ of similar size, F1 under O is explained by simple mixing, and the conclusion rests on P.

### 7.7 Secondary analyses (pre-registered, not used for the F1 decision)
- all hooks and their concatenation;
- H4 with a checkpoint-order permutation null;
- H0 checks;
- probes (S7): ridge regression of activations onto $b^{(32)}$, compared against $r_w$ and against shuffled labels;
- circular coordinates on the cylinder compared with the latent x angle (S7);
- one-factor-at-a-time sensitivity (S7).
