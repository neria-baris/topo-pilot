## Question, competing explanations and new justification

Do controlled changes along a learned phase-associated centroid curve produce lower regret against an independently specified counterfactual oracle prediction than prospectively matched changes whose direction is randomized?

This is a phase-associated centroid-intervention question at the original circular-analysis site resid_post_3/p32 in the three existing E4 cylinder-O models1729/2718/3141, step5000. At this final residual site it concerns the trained readout's response to the component, not how earlier blocks computed it. A positive result would not establish H1 necessity, a literal internal circle variable, causal long memory or relevance to natural-language models.

The new discrimination is directional state forcing, rather than erasure: compare an oracle-defined semantic change to randomized changes of matched magnitude. Competing explanations are (a) the circular component has a directionally meaningful effect on prediction; (b) apparent effects are explained by generic displacement, changes to non-target information or off-support states. Matching/support gates make (b) a testable alternative; passing them is not proof that every unmeasured nuisance is controlled.

[X4-F's accepted closure](X4F_COORDINATOR_REVIEW_ADDENDUM.md) remains INCONCLUSIVE/FAMILY_UNMATCHED, causal endpointNOT_RUN. Its rank2 eraser only removed linear recoverability; fixedRBF recovery remained.998. No additional random draws/site substitution/retuning under that protocol. This new design neither claims that its old family was impossible nor changes its verdict. [Old gate report](X4F_FEASIBILITY_GATES_REPORT.md) must be read with its governing addendum.

There is exactly one transition after the posterior, then emission, for next-symbol prediction. qD is recomputed from H', not assumed to be a rotated qS: retaining the last symbol breaks the full-prefix translation identity.
3. H and H' are both valid positive-likelihood prefixes, but H' is a constructed counterfactual and need not have the natural source-history distribution. Oracle-likelihood/support gates must address implausible constructed donors; positivity alone is insufficient.

Proposed fixed construction, to be independently reviewed before implementation:
- Calibration data only: freeze chart phi, its calibration gauge, support rules and exact artifact hashes. Evaluate source/donor phases by the same frozen transform. Do not orient with validation/test regret or donor-response endpoints.
- Fit an activation nuisance mean L(v) using intercept,36 last-symbol indicators and oracle posterior y-mean/y-second-moment. Standardize the two continuous features on calibration; ridge coefficient10^-4 with the intercept unpenalized. This is a fitted nuisance adjustment, not a claim of complete conditional independence.
- On residuals a-L(v), fit12 equally spaced circular-bin centroids (half-open bins, last wraps); require at least32 calibration rows in EVERY bin. Interpolate periodically and linearly between bin centres to obtain mu(phi). No outcomes, decoder logits or donor qD enter that fit. This phase-dependent curve is nonlinear in activation space; it is not a linear eraser and does not claim to remove all circle information.
- For source activation aS, sourcephasephiS and donorphasephiD, intervention aT=aS+mu(phiD)-mu(phiS). Retain the recipient prefix and all other positions. Use the existing learner.forward_with_patch atresid_post_3,tokenindex32 after[BOS,w1..w32]; weights/buffers/evalmode unchanged. Verify identity patch exactly reproduces the unpatched pass.
- Full donor-state replacement is an engineering positive control only: at this final residual site it trivially reproduces the donor's final readout, so it cannot establish circle-specific causality and is excluded from the primary endpoint. Identity and zero-shift patches are negative controls.

If this construction fails to move the independently assessed circular coordinate toward its prescribed donor value while preserving declared nuisance information, it has failed to implement the intended intervention. Never treat a large output change alone as successful state forcing.

## Randomized family and early matching gate

Freeze32 random sign fields before validation outcomes: independent PCG64 seeds520201..520232, paired across models by canonical sequence_id. For each row let d=wrap(phiD-phiS)in(-pi,pi]. A control requests phasephiS+epsilon*abs(d),epsilon in{-1,+1} with equal probabilities, through the SAME nonlinear centroid curve. It knows the displacement magnitude, not its semantic direction; some random signs will agree with the primary. For each row there are only TWO requested phase destinations, and approximately half of the32 signs reproduce the primary direction exactly. Those coincidences are retained and reported. Thus controls measure the average of the intended direction and its reflection, not a universally wrong direction,32 independent semantic alternatives or a zero-response null. This attenuation must inform the proposed margin/power interpretation.

Scale each nonzero control displacement to the primary's Euclidean norm. If the primary norm iszero, both displacements arezero. A zero control displacement with nonzero primary is unmatched and stops the gate. Scaling is solely geometric and frozen; it can distort the requested phase, which the transfer/damage gate must detect. It is not evidence of matching by itself.

No acceptance/rejection of random fields, regeneration or increasing draws. Assess ALL32 fields on cal and independent val, before intervention prediction endpoints. Proposed prefreeze tolerances:
- Exact per-row patch-norm equality within a propagated relative numerical bound1e-8; compare both raw and calibration-whitened norms, with whitened relative mismatch<=5% perrow.
- Each field's centred displacement covariance trace differs from the primary by<=5% of the primary trace on each split. Degenerate trace requires exactzero forboth; no guessed denominator floor.
- Independent diagnostic circle readout (a fixed calibration-fit nonlinear probe, held-out validation) verifies primary mean circular-angle error to its prescribed donor phase<=pi/12 and95thpercentile<=pi/6. It is a manipulation check, not the learned chart's definition or proof of complete information transfer.
- For every random field, the mean squared change of independently decoded FIRST-HARMONIC circle state from the source matches the primary within5%, plus its variance within10%. This is a decoded-state DISPLACEMENT metric, not retained-information damage. A separate retained-information gate is required below; displacement matching alone cannot satisfy the owner requirement. Near-zero posterior vectors require prespecified operational handling; do not convert a tiny magnitude to an arbitrary precise angle.
- Nuisance preservation: calibration-fit fixed nonlinear probes of posterior y-mean/y-second-moment and last-symbol labels; evaluate degradation on val. Primary and each control must differ by<=.02 normalizedMSE (continuous) and<=.02 accuracy loss(last-symbol). Report absolute losses, not just primary-minus-control. This is bounded measured recoverability damage, not mutual information or total information loss.
- All patched states must satisfy the frozen fitted-chart support rules and a calibration-reference Mahalanobis-distance99thpercentile limit, reported separately. No trimming unsupported rows, complete-case endpoint, fallback intervention or phase refit.

Additional independent-review requirements for the owner information-damage condition:
- Fit separate fixed nonlinear probes AFTER each intervention on patched CALIBRATION activations to recover the ORIGINAL source first-harmonic target, then evaluate on independent validation. Compare the primary and every random field's held-out loss of recoverability versus unpatched activations, using a proposed normalizedMSE difference bound.02. Report absolute degradation and residual recoverability; no claim of complete erasure or mutual-information matching. Probe architecture/hyperparameters must be fixed independently before implementation (currently an explicit blocker).
- Assess posterior circle radius and second/third harmonics, and the FULL12-state y-marginal, not just its first two moments. Distinguish phase-associated changes (part of this centroid intervention) from unintended nuisance changes. Specify source/donor oracle differences and signed nuisance shifts, not just equal probe degradation. A preregistered compatibility/support rule must identify whether the proposed counterfactual is actually representable by this intervention; if not, reformulate before fresh data. Do not call this first-harmonic-only or semantically isolated state forcing.
These are proposed DESIGN CHOICES, not empirical guarantees or known attainable bounds. In particular norm scaling need not preserve whitened norm, covariance or phase damage. The independent reviewer must judge whether these gates separate relevant alternatives and are operationally attainable. Any mismatch stops this formulation as FAMILY_UNMATCHED/INCONCLUSIVE; no outcome-informed tuning.

## Headroom first, validation precision, and one fresh endpoint

Before any activation extraction/patching, compute on source/donor PUBLIC histories the oracle directional headroom G=E KL(qD||qS), with likelihood/representation identities verified. Proposed requirement: a one-sided95% lower boundG>.005nat and EVERY donor's final-symbol surprise under its pre-w32 posterior<=10nats. Those are scope/design choices requiring review, not universal thresholds. This is an oracle-only gate; a failure stops before models. Distinct source/donor processes or changed test distributions are not silently substituted.

For modelj define counterfactual regret R(qD,p)=KL(qD||p), p from its frozen model/patch pass. For each source trajectory:

Y_j = [R(qD,pS)-R(qD,pT)] - mean_r[R(qD,pS)-R(qD,pC_r)]
    = mean_r R(qD,pC_r) - R(qD,pT).

Primary population endpoint theta_j=E Y_j / E KL(qD||qS). The denominator is oracle directional headroom; this is excess directional transfer over the fixed matched randomized family, not erasure necessity. Because the unpatched baseline cancels algebraically, a positive theta does NOT establish improvement over the original prediction: the primary may worsen donor regret while the controls worsen it more. Report primary improvement R(qD,pS)-R(qD,pT) separately with intervals as a descriptive secondary quantity. The primary claim is RELATIVE directional advantage only, never successful predictive repair. No sampled next token is needed: use exact expectation under qD. Retain finite log-softmax probabilities and analytic numerical-error certificates; never silently probability-floor logits.

Proposed practical criterion: theta_j>.10 in EVERY original model. Joint whole-source-trajectory bootstrap5000draws, PCG64seed520303; include BOTH source/donor, allcontrols and allmodels in the same draw, retaining numerator/denominator pairing. Bonferroni one-sided lower quantile.05/3, inverted_cdf; report central95%intervals and upperbounds descriptively. Control randomness is frozen for this CONDITIONAL endpoint; claims do not average over all possible fitted controls/charts/seedpopulations.

Before fresh-test release, validation-only precision must project>=80%power at alternative theta=.20 versusmargin.10 with the locked sample size. A ratio endpoint needs a validated ratio/delta-method or centred-empirical projection that resamples numerator/denominator jointly; the K4 mean-only precision function cannot be reused blindly. At freeze choose ONE method, formula, seed and exact pass rule by independent mathematical review, before observing any fresh data. This unresolved precision specification currently BLOCKS preregistration/execution.

Proposed separatecal/val/fresh counts4096/2048/4096 source trajectories, generated by the existing sampler and isolated lineage, process seed maps fixed in the final reviewed contract. Existing matching original-site cal/val caches may replace duplicate extraction ONLY if their exact source/split/hook/checkpoint/independence identities meet this design; never use exposed oldtest as fresh. Fresh history/ObservationSet/activation/logit/endpoint artifacts remain inaccessible until protocol+implementation+allval gates are accepted. No extra sample after seeing fresh results. All rows enter the endpoint or the cohort stops; define how unexpected fresh support/matching failure yieldsINCONCLUSIVE BEFORE mean scoring.

Interpretation: lower>.10allmodels->support for relative directional advantage of this phase-associated centroid intervention, conditional on the declared gates. Upper<.10foranymodel->scoped lack of practical support; othercasesINCONCLUSIVE. Passing is not universal coordinate necessity, all-history functional use or topology necessity. Failure of manipulation/matching/support/headroom is assay infeasibility, not rejection of causal use.
